[SECTION: ABOUT STREAMKAR]
Q: What is StreamKar?
A: StreamKar is India's top 24x7 live streaming app. Users can watch broadcasters, showcase their own talent (singing, dancing, chatting), make friends via video chat, and earn money by receiving virtual gifts.

[SECTION: ACCOUNT & LOGIN]
Q: How do I sign up?
A: You can use:
1. Facebook
2. Instagram
3. Google Account
4. Phone Number (OTP)

Q: How do I customize my profile?
A: Go to your Profile Page. Click your picture to change the avatar. Click 'Edit' to change your nickname or bio. You can also link multiple social media accounts here.

[SECTION: STREAMING & FEATURES]
Q: How do I start streaming?
A:
1. Tap the central Camera/Video icon on the home screen.
2. Select a tag (e.g., Dance, Music, Talk).
3. Add a title and cover image.
4. Tap "Go Live". You can use beauty filters during the stream.

Q: What are PK Matches?
A: A PK (Player Kill) match is a live battle between two streamers.
- The streamer with the most points (gifts) wins.
- The loser must perform a punishment task.
- This increases viewer engagement and earnings.

Q: What is a Guardian?
A: A Guardian is a loyal supporter of a specific streamer. They get a special badge and entrance effect in that streamer's room to show their high status and protection.

Q: What is the VIP System?
A: VIP is a paid subscription that gives you:
- Exclusive entrance effects (driving a luxury car into the room).
- Special chat bubbles.
- A unique badge next to your name.
- Higher status in the viewer list.

[SECTION: EARNINGS & SALARY]
Q: How do I earn money?
A: You earn "Gems" when viewers send you gifts (Beans).
- Gems can be converted to real cash.
- You can withdraw your salary via bank transfer or Payoneer.
- Official hosts (signed with an agency) get extra bonuses for hitting monthly targets.

[SECTION: TECHNICAL SUPPORT]
Q: The app is crashing or lagging.
A:
1. Check your internet (WiFi/4G).
2. Clear the app cache in Settings.
3. Update to the latest version from the App Store/Play Store.
4. Restart your phone.

Q: I have a payment issue.
A: If you bought Beans but didn't receive them, email 'payment@streamkar.com' with your User ID and transaction screenshot.
