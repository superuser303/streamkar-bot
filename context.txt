[SECTION: ACCOUNT CREATION & MANAGEMENT]
Q: How do I create a StreamKar account?
A: You can sign up for StreamKar using three methods:
1. Mobile Number: Enter your phone number and verify via OTP.
2. Facebook: Link your Facebook account for one-click login.
3. Google: Use your Gmail account for quick access.
4. Quick Login: Use the 'Guest' mode (not recommended for long-term use as data may be lost).

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: If you registered via phone number:
1. Go to the Login page.
2. Click "Forgot Password?"
3. Enter your registered mobile number.
4. Enter the OTP sent to your phone.
5. Create a new password.
Note: If you use Facebook or Google, you must reset your password through those respective platforms.

Q: How can I change my profile picture or nickname?
A: Go to your Profile Page > Click 'Edit' (Pencil Icon). You can upload a new photo and change your nickname. Note that nickname changes may be limited to once every certain period or may require a VIP level.

Q: How do I delete my StreamKar account?
A: To delete your account, go to Settings > Account Security > Delete Account. Please note this action is irreversible. All your Beans, Gems, and data will be permanently lost.

[SECTION: STREAMING FEATURES]
Q: What is a PK Battle?
A: A PK (Player Kill) Battle is a real-time competition between two streamers. The streamer who receives the most gifts (Beans) during the timer wins. The loser usually has to perform a punishment task chosen by the winner.

Q: What is the difference between Audio and Video streaming?
A: 
- Video Streaming: The standard mode where viewers can see and hear you.
- Audio Streaming: A radio-style mode where only your voice is heard. This is ideal for users with lower bandwidth or those who prefer privacy.

Q: How do I start a broadcast?
A: Tap the large circular '+' or 'Video' icon at the bottom of the home screen. Add a title, select a tag (e.g., Music, Chat, Dance), and tap "Go Live."

Q: What is the Multi-Guest feature?
A: Multi-Guest allows a host to invite other users into their stream.
- Audio Room: Up to 8 guests can join the panel.
- Video Room: Usually supports 1-2 guests in a split-screen format.

[SECTION: ECONOMY & GIFTING]
Q: What are Beans and Gems?
A:
- Beans: This is the currency users buy to send gifts to streamers.
- Gems: This is what streamers receive when they get gifts. Gems can be converted into real money (salary) or exchanged back into Beans.

Q: How do I recharge Beans?
A: You can recharge via:
1. The App: Go to Profile > Wallet > Recharge.
2. Official Website: Visit www.streamkar.com and select 'Recharge' for often better rates and more payment options (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Google Pay, etc.).
3. Authorized Resellers: Contact official agency resellers.

Q: How do I withdraw my salary?
A: To withdraw, you must have a minimum balance of Gems (usually equivalent to $10 or more). Go to Profile > Earnings > Withdraw. You must link a valid payment method like Payoneer or a local bank account depending on your region.

[SECTION: TECHNICAL SUPPORT]
Q: My app is lagging or freezing. What should I do?
A: 
1. Check your internet connection. A stable speed of at least 5 Mbps is recommended.
2. Clear the app cache: Go to Profile > Settings > Clear Cache.
3. Check for updates in the Play Store or App Store.
4. Restart your device.

Q: I cannot hear any audio in the stream.
A:
1. Check if your phone is on "Silent" mode or if media volume is down.
2. Ensure you haven't blocked the host.
3. Exit the room and re-enter.

Q: I am unable to login. It says "Network Error".
A:
1. Switch between Wi-Fi and Mobile Data.
2. If you are using a VPN, turn it off. StreamKar may block suspicious VPN IPs.
3. If the server is under maintenance, wait for an official announcement on StreamKar social media.

Q: How do I report a user for abusive behavior?
A: Tap the user's profile picture > Click the '...' (Option menu) > Select 'Report'. Choose the reason (Harassment, Spam, Inappropriate Content). Screenshots are helpful.

[SECTION: AGENCY & HOSTING]
Q: How do I become an official host?
A: You typically need to join an Agency. Agencies recruit talent, train them, and help process salaries. You cannot usually become a paid official host without an agency.

Q: What are the benefits of being an official host?
A: Official hosts receive a monthly salary based on their "Target" (a quota of Gems received and valid streaming hours). They also get more exposure on the 'Hot' page.
